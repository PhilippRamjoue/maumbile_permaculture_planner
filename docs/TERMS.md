## 1. What Maumbile is

Maumbile is a **free, non-commercial public-good tool** for planning permaculture sites on a map.
It is open-source software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](../LICENSE). The hosted instance is run by an
individual as a free service for everyone, especially in lower-income regions. It has no ads, no paid
tier and no sale of data.

satellite imagery and ~30 m elevation data, so they are **approximate**. Check them on the ground
before you build, dig, divert water or rely on a boundary. Imagery can be outdated or misaligned.

- You need an account (an email address and a password) to save sites and designs. Keep your
  password private; you are responsible for activity under your account.
- **Your designs are yours.** You give the operator only the permission needed to store them and to
  show them to you, and to anyone you give a share link to.
- Do not put unlawful content or other people's personal data in your sites and plans.
- You can **export** every plan as GeoJSON at any time, and can **delete** your data (see
  [Privacy](PRIVACY.md)). Keep your own copies: this is a free service without a backup guarantee.

## 4. Acceptable use

Do not attack, overload or probe the service, do not try to access other people's data, and do not use
the imagery proxy to bulk-download map tiles. The operator may limit or suspend accounts that do.

## 5. Third-party map and elevation data

Maumbile shows data from other providers, each under **its own license and terms**: Esri World
Imagery, Sentinel-2 cloudless (EOX / Copernicus), Copernicus DEM, OpenStreetMap and others. They are
listed with their attributions in the app (**Data sources & credits**) and in
[`DATA_SOURCES.md`](DATA_SOURCES.md). Keep the attribution when you share exports.

Esri World Imagery is the default because it has the best resolution in the project's target regions,
and is used here for a free, non-commercial public-good service. **If you use Maumbile or its outputs
commercially** (for example in paid consulting, products or services), **you are responsible for
obtaining the licenses those providers require.** Esri imagery in particular needs its own license for
commercial use; a license-clean alternative (Sentinel-2 cloudless) is available when you self-host.
